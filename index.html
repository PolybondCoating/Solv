GOVERNANCE

Method & limits

What PowderSolve does

  • Starts with the user's plain-language symptom.
  • Suggests the closest documented problem.
  • Asks a few diagnostic questions — each answer can change the next question.
  • Shows causes, verification tests and conditional corrective actions.
  • Keeps source and review information separate.

What it does not do

  • Authorize a production change.
  • Replace a product TDS/SDS or equipment manual.
  • Turn reference cure curves into universal recipes.
  • Declare a root cause without verification.
The knowledge base contains structured draft records and source-attributed material. Technical review status is shown in the underlying records and should be completed before controlled production release.

Safety gate

  • Follow the equipment manufacturer’s manual, SDS and site risk assessment.
  • Do not bypass grounding, interlocks, ventilation, explosion protection or lockout/tagout controls.
  • Do not make cure changes without confirming product-specific minimum and maximum exposure guidance.
  • Stop and escalate for fire, electrical, compressed-air, solvent, dust or unknown chemical hazards.
